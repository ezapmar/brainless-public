# {{date:YYYY}} Annual Review (40 Questions)

> Adapted from Steph Ango's `kepano/40-questions` template for `brainless`.
> File completed reviews under `Thinking/` or `Personal/` (marked `privacy: personal` so they compile locally only).

## 40 Questions to Ask Yourself Every Year

1. What did you do this year that you'd never done before?
2. Did you keep your new year's resolutions?
3. Did anyone close to you give birth?
4. Did anyone close to you die?
5. What cities/states/countries did you visit?
6. What would you like to have next year that you lacked this year?
7. What date(s) from this year will remain etched upon your memory, and why?
8. What was your biggest achievement of the year?
9. What was your biggest failure?
10. What other hardships did you face?
11. Did you suffer illness or injury?
12. What was the best thing you bought?
13. Whose behaviour merited celebration?
14. Whose behaviour made you appalled?
15. Where did most of your money go?
16. What did you get really, really, really excited about?
17. What song will always remind you of this year?
18. Compared to this time last year, are you: happier or sadder? Thinner or fatter? Richer or poorer?
19. What do you wish you'd done more of?
20. What do you wish you'd done less of?
21. How are you spending the holidays?
22. Did you fall in love this year?
23. Do you hate anyone now that you didn't hate this time last year?
24. What was your favourite show?
25. What was the best book you read?
26. What was your greatest musical discovery of the year?
27. What was your favourite film?
28. What was your favourite meal?
29. What did you want and get?
30. What did you want and not get?
31. What did you do on your birthday?
32. What one thing would have made your year immeasurably more satisfying?
33. How would you describe your personal fashion this year?
34. What kept you sane?
35. Which celebrity/public figure did you admire the most?
36. What political issue stirred you the most?
37. Who did you miss?
38. Who was the best new person you met?
39. What valuable life lesson did you learn this year?
40. What is a quote that sums up your year?

## 40 Questions to Ask Yourself Every Decade

1. What would you do if you had 10 billion dollars?
2. What would you do if you had 1 million dollars?
3. What would you do if you did not have to work for a living?
4. What do you love doing?
5. What are you good at?
6. What gives you energy?
7. What drains your energy?
8. What are you most afraid of?
9. What are you procrastinating on?
10. What is holding you back?
11. What bad habits do you want to break?
12. What good habits do you want to cultivate?
13. What would you regret not doing?
14. What do you want to be remembered for?
15. Who do you admire? Why?
16. Who makes you feel like your best self?
17. What are your core values?
18. What does a great day look like for you?
19. What does a great year look like for you?
20. Where do you want to live?
21. What skills do you want to master?
22. What topics do you want to explore deeply?
23. What projects do you want to create?
24. What relationships do you want to invest in?
25. What boundaries do you need to set?
26. What beliefs have you outgrown?
27. What have you changed your mind about?
28. What advice would you give your younger self?
29. What advice would your older self give you now?
30. What does enough look like for you?
31. How do you define success?
32. How do you define happiness?
33. What are you optimising for right now?
34. What trade-offs are you willing to make?
35. What trade-offs are you no longer willing to make?
36. What is one bold bet worth making in the next 10 years?
37. What would you do if you knew you could not fail?
38. What would you do even if you were sure to fail?
39. What keeps you curious?
40. What kind of person do you want to become?
